ND7 in Parkinson’s Disease — Proposal Figure Notes

Overall Hypothesis

The proposal tests ND7, a 98-amino-acid C-terminal fragment of progranulin (PGRN), as a potential disease-modifying treatment for Parkinson’s disease (PD).

The proposed biological model is:

ND7 → crosses BBB → interacts with/supports GCase → improves lysosomal function → reduces lipid accumulation and neuroinflammation → reduces neurodegeneration → improves PD-related phenotypes

The proposal contains two major aims:

  • Aim 1: Test ND7 in aged PG9V mice, a GBA1/PGRN-associated model with neuropathic Gaucher disease (nGD) and PD-like phenotypes.
  • Aim 2: Test ND7 in human α-synuclein A53T (G2-3) transgenic mice, a genetic PD model.

Preliminary Data

Figure 1 — Development and Characterization of ND7

Main question

What is ND7, and does it retain the important biological activity of full-length PGRN?

Figure 1A — ND7 structure and sequence

ND7 is derived from the C-terminal 98 amino acids of PGRN (aa 496–593) and contains the granulin E (Grn E) domain.

A series of PGRN deletion constructs was tested to identify the region required for interaction with GCase (glucocerebrosidase).

The C-terminal 98-aa region was found to be necessary and sufficient for GCase binding and was named ND7, meaning the seventh N-terminal deletion construct.

Figure 1B — ND7 protein production

Recombinant ND7 was characterized using:

  • Coomassie blue staining
  • Western blot using anti-His antibody

This verifies that recombinant His-tagged ND7 protein was successfully produced.

Figure 1C — Protein-binding assay

ND7 was tested for interaction with proteins involved in PGRN/lysosomal biology.

The proposal states that ND7 binds:

  • GCase
  • HSP70
  • Sortilin

The important interaction for the proposal is:

ND7 ↔︎ GCase

This supports the idea that ND7 retains the GCase-related function of PGRN.

Figure 1D — Functional effect in human GD fibroblasts

ND7 was tested in fibroblasts from a patient with type 2 Gaucher disease carrying GBA1 D409H.

ND7 treatment reduced lysosomal storage.

Main conclusion

ND7 retains the GCase-binding and lysosomal activity of PGRN despite being only a small C-terminal PGRN fragment.

This establishes the molecule that will be used throughout the rest of the proposal.

Figure 2 — ND7 Crosses the BBB and Increases GCase Activity

Main question

Can systemically administered ND7 reach the brain and remain biologically functional?

The experiment used:

  • Gba1 D409V/null mice
  • 2 months old
  • His-tagged ND7
  • 16 mg/kg
  • Intraperitoneal (IP) injection

These mice have very low residual GCase activity.

Figure 2A — Distribution of ND7

After injection, anti-His staining was used to detect His-tagged ND7.

ND7 was detected in visceral organs including:

  • Lung
  • Liver
  • Spleen

Importantly, ND7 was also detected in the brain, including:

  • Cortex
  • Cerebellum
  • Hippocampus

Interpretation

Detection of systemically administered ND7 in brain tissue provides evidence that ND7 can reach the CNS.

Figure 2B — Liver GCase activity

GCase activity increased in the liver after ND7 treatment.

Figure 2C — Brain GCase activity

GCase activity also increased in the brain approximately 0.5–2 hours after injection.

This is especially important because it suggests that ND7 detected in the CNS is functionally associated with increased GCase activity.

Main conclusion

Systemically administered ND7 reaches the brain and increases brain GCase activity.

This provides the pharmacological rationale for using ND7 to treat neurological disease.

Figure 3 — ND7 Penetrates Brain Tissue Better Than Full-Length PGRN

Main question

Is brain penetration a special advantage of ND7 compared with full-length PGRN?

Experimental design

PGRN knockout (Grn-/-) mice received:

  • Full-length PGRN, or
  • ND7

Dose:

100 µg by IP injection

Brains were collected 24 hours later.

A PGRN antibody capable of recognizing both PGRN and ND7 was used for staining.

Result

The proposal reports detectable signal in brain tissue after ND7 treatment, whereas comparable signal was not detected after full-length PGRN treatment.

Main conclusion

ND7 can penetrate brain tissue, whereas full-length PGRN showed little/no detectable brain penetration under these experimental conditions.

Why this matters

Full-length PGRN has potentially useful biological functions, but its therapeutic application has limitations.

ND7 potentially provides:

PGRN-derived lysosomal activity + better CNS accessibility

Figure 4 — PG9V Mice Develop CNS Inflammation and PD-Like Pathology

Main question

Does the PG9V mouse provide an appropriate model for testing ND7 against PD-like pathology?

Mouse model

PG9V mice were generated by combining:

Gba1 mutation + Pgrn deficiency

The proposal describes PG9V as a model showing severe neuronopathic Gaucher disease and PD-like features.

Figure 4A — Astrogliosis

Brain sections from approximately 12-month-old PG9V mice were stained for:

GFAP

GFAP is a commonly used marker of reactive astrocytes.

Increased GFAP therefore indicates:

astrocyte activation / astrogliosis

Figure 4B — α-Synuclein pathology

Brains from:

  • WT
  • Gba9V/9V
  • Grn-/-
  • PG9V

were examined for α-synuclein.

The proposal reports enhanced α-synuclein accumulation in PG9V brain tissue.

It also describes accumulation of phosphorylated α-synuclein (Ser129) as part of the PD-like phenotype.

Main conclusion

PG9V mice show:

GBA1/PGRN dysfunction → CNS inflammation + α-synuclein pathology

Therefore, aged PG9V mice provide the disease model for Specific Aim 1.

Figure 5 — ND7 Reduces Lysosomal Storage in PG9V Mice

Main question

Can ND7 correct Gaucher-related lysosomal abnormalities in vivo?

Experimental design

Three-month-old PG9V mice were challenged with ovalbumin (OVA) and treated with:

  • PBS
  • Recombinant PGRN
  • ND7

Figure 5A — Recombinant proteins

Recombinant proteins were characterized by Coomassie blue staining.

This is primarily a protein preparation/quality-control component.

Figure 5B — Lysosomal ultrastructure

Lung tissue was examined by:

TEM — transmission electron microscopy

The experiment visualized abnormal tubular-like lysosomal structures associated with lysosomal storage pathology.

Figure 5C — Quantification

The investigators quantified:

  • Number of tubular lysosomes
  • Size of tubular lysosomes

Both PGRN and ND7 reduced lysosomal abnormalities.

Main conclusion

ND7 can reproduce an important therapeutic effect of full-length PGRN and reduce lysosomal storage pathology in vivo.

Figure 6 — ND7 Treats Neuropathic Phenotypes in Aged PG9V Mice

Main question

Can ND7 treat CNS disease in PG9V mice after neurological pathology has developed?

Experimental design

10-month-old PG9V mice received:

  • PBS
  • ND7
  • Imiglucerase

ND7:

1 mg/kg IP, 3 times/week for 2 months

Imiglucerase is recombinant GCase used clinically as enzyme replacement therapy for Gaucher disease.

Figure 6A — GCase activity

GCase activity was measured in:

  • Liver
  • Brain

ND7 significantly increased brain GCase activity.

Imiglucerase did not produce a comparable increase in brain GCase activity.

Why?

The proposal interprets this difference in terms of CNS accessibility: recombinant GCase used for conventional ERT does not effectively reach the brain.

Figure 6B — GluCer accumulation

GluCer/GC = glucosylceramide

It is a direct substrate of GCase.

When GCase activity is deficient:

GCase ↓ → GluCer degradation ↓ → GluCer accumulation ↑

ND7 reduced brain GluCer accumulation.

Therefore:

ND7 → GCase activity ↑ → GluCer ↓

Figure 6C — Microgliosis

Brain sections were stained for:

Iba1

Iba1 is used here as a marker of microglial activation.

ND7 reduced microgliosis.

Figure 6D — Astrogliosis

Brain sections were stained for:

GFAP

ND7 reduced astrocyte activation/astrogliosis.

Main conclusion

ND7 affects multiple levels of the proposed disease pathway:

ND7 → brain GCase ↑ → lipid storage ↓ → microgliosis/astrogliosis ↓

This is one of the strongest preliminary figures supporting Aim 1.

MPTP Parkinson’s Disease Experiments

Figure 7 — ND7 Improves Motor Behavior in MPTP-Induced PD

Main question

The PG9V model is closely connected to GBA1/PGRN biology.

Therefore, the investigators asked:

Can ND7 also work in a PD model that is not created by GBA1/PGRN mutation?

MPTP model

C57BL/6J mice were treated with MPTP.

MPTP is converted to the neurotoxic metabolite MPP+, which ultimately damages dopaminergic neurons through mitochondrial dysfunction.

The proposal describes animals receiving MPTP with or without PGRN or ND7 treatment.

Readout

Open-field/gait trajectory analysis

MPTP-treated animals showed impaired movement.

ND7-treated animals showed improved gait trajectories.

Main conclusion

ND7 improves a functional motor phenotype in chemically induced PD.

This is important because the effect is not restricted to the PG9V genetic background.

Figure 8 — ND7 Reduces Astrogliosis in MPTP-Induced PD

Main question

Does the behavioral improvement in Figure 7 correspond to reduced CNS inflammation?

Marker

GFAP = glial fibrillary acidic protein

It is used to identify astrocyte activation.

Tissue regions

The figure examines multiple brain regions, including:

  • Hippocampus
  • Midbrain
  • Cerebellum

Result

MPTP increases astrocyte activation.

ND7 treatment markedly reduces GFAP-associated astrogliosis.

Main conclusion

ND7 suppresses neuroinflammatory astrocyte activation in the MPTP PD model.

This connects the behavioral effect in Figure 7 with a pathological CNS effect.

Figure 9 — ND7 Protects Neurons in MPTP-Induced PD

Main question

Does ND7 actually protect neurons, rather than only reducing inflammation or changing behavior?

Figure 9A — Western blot

The experiment measures:

  • NeuN
  • TH
  • GAPDH as loading control

NeuN

NeuN = neuronal nuclear protein

It is widely used as a neuronal marker.

Higher NeuN after ND7 treatment is interpreted as better preservation of neurons.

TH

TH = tyrosine hydroxylase

TH is a key enzyme required for dopamine synthesis and is commonly used as a marker of dopaminergic neurons.

MPTP damages dopaminergic neurons.

Therefore:

MPTP → TH ↓

The proposal reports that:

ND7 → TH ↑ relative to MPTP controls

Figure 9B — Quantification

Western blot signals for NeuN and TH are quantified.

ND7 increases both markers relative to the MPTP/control treatment conditions described in the proposal.

Main conclusion

ND7 is associated with preservation of neurons and dopaminergic neuronal markers in MPTP-induced PD.

Figures 7–9 therefore form one logical experiment:

Figure 7: behavior improves
↓
Figure 8: neuroinflammation decreases
↓
Figure 9: neuronal loss decreases

Proposed Experiments

Figure 10 — Specific Aim 1 Experimental Design

Main question

Can ND7 prevent or treat established PD-like pathology in aged PG9V mice?

This is a planned study, not a preliminary-result figure.

Mouse model

PG9V

Two treatment strategies are proposed.

Prevention study

Start treatment:

10 months old

Continue approximately:

4 months

The purpose is to intervene before severe PD-like symptoms.

Therapeutic study

Start treatment:

14 months old

This tests whether ND7 can improve disease after pronounced PD-like pathology has developed.

Treatment groups

  1. PBS
  2. PGRN — 5 mg/kg
  3. ND7 — 1 mg/kg
  4. Imiglucerase — 2.5 U/kg

Treatment frequency:

3 times/week

Animals

Each treatment group:

n = 16

  • 8 male
  • 8 female

Behavioral outcomes

Measure:

  • Lifespan
  • Hind-limb clasping
  • Gait/open-field behavior
  • Repeated open field
  • Y-maze

PD/neurodegeneration outcomes

Measure:

  • α-Syn
  • β-Amyloid
  • Tau
  • TDP-43

using:

  • IHC
  • IF
  • Western blot

Neuroinflammation

Astrocytes:

GFAP

Microglia/macrophages:

  • Iba1
  • CD68

Inflammatory genes:

  • TNFα
  • IL1β
  • Ccl2/5
  • Cxcl1/2/10
  • Ccr5

GCase pathway

Measure:

GCase activity

and its substrates:

  • GC/GluCer
  • GS/GluSph

in CNS and visceral organs.

Visceral Gaucher phenotypes

Measure:

  • Liver/body-weight ratio
  • Spleen/body-weight ratio
  • Lung/body-weight ratio
  • Gaucher cells
  • Tubular lysosomes by TEM
  • Tissue inflammation

Overall logic

If ND7 is disease modifying, the expected pattern is:

ND7 ↓
GCase activity ↑ ↓
GC + GS accumulation ↓ ↓
lysosomal dysfunction ↓ ↓
α-Syn/neurodegenerative pathology ↓ ↓
microgliosis + astrogliosis ↓ ↓
neuronal dysfunction ↓ ↓
motor/cognitive function ↑

Specific Aim 2

Figure 11 — A53T α-Synuclein PD Study Design

Main question

Aim 1 asks whether ND7 works in GBA1/PGRN-associated disease.

Aim 2 asks a broader question:

Can ND7 treat α-synuclein-driven PD even when the disease is not created by the PG9V genotype?

Mouse model

Human α-synuclein A53T transgenic mice — G2-3 line

These mice overexpress human α-synuclein carrying the PD-associated:

A53T mutation

The proposal states that these mice develop:

  • Progressive motor abnormalities
  • α-Syn pathology
  • Neuronal abnormalities/loss

Prevention study

Treatment begins around:

9 months

This is before severe symptoms.

Therapeutic study

Treatment begins around:

12 months

This tests treatment after disease has progressed further.

Treatment groups

  1. PBS
  2. PGRN — 5 mg/kg
  3. ND7 — 1 mg/kg
  4. L-DOPA — 8 mg/kg

Animals

Only male A53T mice are proposed because the proposal states that females show resistance to disease progression.

n = 8/group

Behavioral assessment

  • Y-maze
  • Repeated open-field testing
  • Motor-function assessment

Baseline testing occurs before treatment.

Repeated assessment is proposed approximately every two weeks.

α-Synuclein pathology

Particularly important:

phosphorylated α-Synuclein Ser129 (pS129)

Other proteins:

  • β-Amyloid
  • Tau
  • TDP-43

Methods:

  • IHC
  • IF
  • WB

α-Synuclein inclusions

Use:

TEM

to examine dense α-synuclein inclusions in neuronal processes.

Neuronal death

Use:

TUNEL staining

in regions including:

  • Brainstem
  • Sensorimotor cortex
  • Spinal cord

Dopaminergic abnormalities

Measure:

TH

using:

  • IHC
  • IF
  • WB
  • qPCR

Neuroinflammation

Measure:

  • Astrocyte activation
  • Microglial activation
  • Inflammatory gene expression

Mitochondrial abnormalities

Measure:

  • Drp1
  • PGC-1α
  • Clp

and examine mitochondrial ultrastructure using EM.

GCase/lysosomal biology

Measure:

  • GCase activity
  • GC
  • GS
  • Lysosomal markers

in CNS and visceral tissues.

Overall logic

This aim tests whether the ND7 mechanism extends beyond the PG9V model:

A53T α-Syn accumulation ↓
PD pathology ↓
ND7 treatment ↓
lysosomal/GCase function improves? ↓
α-Syn pathology decreases? ↓
neuroinflammation + mitochondrial dysfunction decrease? ↓
neuronal survival improves? ↓
behavior improves?

If yes, ND7 would have evidence of activity in a broader genetic PD context rather than only GBA1/PGRN-associated disease.

Most Important Take-Home Message

The preliminary data are intended to establish three major points:

  1. ND7 retains PGRN’s useful GCase/lysosomal activity.
  2. ND7 can reach the CNS and improve GCase/lysosomal and inflammatory abnormalities.
  3. ND7 shows beneficial effects in both PG9V and MPTP PD-related models.

The two proposed aims then ask the critical next questions:

Aim 1: Does ND7 prevent and treat established disease in aged PG9V mice?

Aim 2: Does the therapeutic effect generalize to an independent α-synuclein A53T genetic PD model?
